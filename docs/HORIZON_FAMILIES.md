# Companion Quest --- 40 New Companion Families

These are **40 additional original companion families**, each assigned
to a distinct route/trail concept and designed to avoid repeating the
silhouettes, materials, motifs, and identities of the existing roster.

## Art Direction Rules

-   Every family has **Baby → Adolescent → Adult** progression.
-   Each stage must be a **new silhouette**, not a recolor, enlargement,
    or traced variation.
-   Preserve 2--4 unmistakable family traits across all stages so
    evolution reads clearly.
-   Keep the designs friendly enough to be companions. Adult forms can
    be imposing, but should read as guardians, travelers, athletes,
    guides, or natural spirits rather than villains.
-   Use **original anatomy and combinations**. Do not copy, reference,
    imitate, or trace any existing game/anime/franchise creature.
-   For final character art: confident dark outline using a dark version
    of the creature's own palette, hard-edged cel shading, limited
    intentional palette, strong readable silhouette, transparent/clean
    background when needed for asset creation.

## 01. BRINELING → SHOREGLEAM → TIDECROWN

**ROUTE:** Tideglass Coast\
**TRAIL:** Saltglass Strand\
**CORE CONCEPT:** sea-glass hermit spirit\
**PALETTE:** seafoam, aqua, pearl, coral

### BRINELING --- BABY

CREATURE: an original sea-glass hermit spirit. translucent sea-glass
shell, tiny coral feet, pearl eyes. Keep the form compact, immediately
readable and companion-like, with a distinctive face and one signature
visual feature that will persist through the family. It should feel
young, curious and suited to the environment of **Saltglass Strand**.

### SHOREGLEAM --- ADOLESCENT

CREATURE: the adolescent form of Brineling --- larger faceted shell with
tidal ribbons and coral limbs. Preserve the baby's recognizable facial
identity and signature motif, but change the proportions, stance and
silhouette substantially. It should feel more capable and exploratory,
clearly adapted to traveling **Tideglass Coast**, without simply looking
like a larger baby.

### TIDECROWN --- ADULT

CREATURE: the adult form of Shoregleam --- towering sea-glass guardian
with a crown of surf and luminous coral. Carry forward the family's core
motif and recognizable eyes/markings while transforming the silhouette
into a mature, memorable final form. Powerful, composed and protective
rather than evil; it should feel like the definitive guardian or master
traveler of **Saltglass Strand**.

# Roster Summary

This expansion adds **40 families / 120 individual forms**. Each family
is tied to its own route/trail identity and was intentionally built
around a different primary creature/material/environment concept so the
expanded Creature Index feels like exploration across a large world
rather than variants of the same few ideas.
