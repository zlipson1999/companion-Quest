Companion Quest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Companion Quest is a fitness game where real walking and real exercise are the only inputs. This page says plainly what the app records, where it goes, and what never leaves your phone.

Without an account (the normal way to play)

Everything stays on your device. Your steps, distance, workouts, habit logs, and your companion's progress are stored only in the app's local storage on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere. The pedometer and, during outdoor runs, your location are read to measure real movement; location is used for distance in the moment and is not stored or transmitted.

If you sign in (optional)

Signing in with Apple or Google exists to do two things: keep your game safe (your save is stored on our server so a new phone can restore it) and let you compare training with friends you both choose. When you sign in we store:

  • The stable account identifier your sign-in provider gives us, and a display name. We do not receive or store your email address, contacts, or anything else from the provider.
  • Your game save, so it can be restored when you sign in again.
  • Per-day training summaries — date, steps, miles, sessions, sets — and your personal records. Days, never a running total, so numbers can be sanity-checked against what a person can actually do.

Friends see only your training summaries and records, only after you have both agreed, and you can disconnect anyone at any time. Nothing else in your game — your companion, your goal, your items — is ever visible to another person.

The Coach chat

Messages you type to the in-game Coach are sent to our server, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the reply. Coach chats are not used to train models and are not linked to your training log.

The camera (form check)

The mirror's form check shows you your own camera with coaching cues over the top. The video never leaves your device — it is not recorded, stored, or transmitted, and the app works fully if you decline camera access.

Deleting your data

In the game: the Friends screen has Delete my account, which permanently removes your save, your training days, your records, and your friendships from the server. Your local game is untouched. Erasing your local save is under Options.

What we never do

  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no trackers.
  • No selling or sharing data with third parties.
  • No reading your health data from other apps.

Contact
